# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes demonstrating empathy and kindness, respecting differing viewpoints,
giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, accepting responsibility for mistakes, and
focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes sexualised language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention;
trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks; public or private
harassment; publishing others' private information without permission; and other conduct that would
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Specific to this project

- **Challenging an analysis is welcome; challenging a person is not.** Showing that a scenario is
  wrong, or that the system produced overconfident output, is a contribution.
- **No solicitation.** Do not use issues, discussions, or pull requests to promote trading
  services, signal groups, tokens, or investment products.
- **No sharing of individual trades or positions as advice.** This project analyses market
  structure; it is not a venue for telling people what to buy.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer via [GitHub](https://github.com/ElmatadorZ).
Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy respected.
