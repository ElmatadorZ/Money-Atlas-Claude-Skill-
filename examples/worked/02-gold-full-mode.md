# Worked example — FULL mode

**User:** Should I add gold to the portfolio here?

A "should I" question triggers FULL mode: the full Genesis Protocol pipeline
(First Principle → System Thinking → SMC layer), multiple scenarios, and explicit
entry/exit zones. The skill gives the structured decision **inputs** — the human
makes the decision. It does not issue personalized investment advice.

---

**Output (shape):**

> **GOLD — FULL analysis**
>
> **First Principle.** Gold has no yield, so its case is a function of *real*
> yields and confidence in fiat. Strip the narrative: what actually moves it is
> the real-rate path and central-bank demand, not headlines. *(inference — labelled)*
>
> **Observed inputs** *(from your data)*: real 10y yield ≈ −0.4%; central-bank net
> buying positive three quarters running; price consolidating under a prior high.
>
> **SMC structure:** accumulation range holding; no distribution signature yet.
>
> **Scenarios**
> | # | Path | Confidence | Entry zone | Invalidation |
> |---|------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
> | 1 | Break and hold above the prior high on continued negative real yields | ~55% | on the retest of the breakout, not the breakout candle | a close back below the range midpoint |
> | 2 | Range-bound while real yields drift up | ~30% | scale at range low only | range low gives way on volume |
> | 3 | Risk-off spike (exogenous) | ~15% | not chased | fades back into the range within days |
>
> **Position note:** this is scenario structure, not a recommendation to buy. Size
> and the buy/sell/hold decision are yours.
>
> **What would change the read:** a sharp move in real yields, or a distribution
> signature appearing in the SMC layer.

---

**Why it is shaped this way**

- The First Principle step is labelled inference, kept separate from the observed
  inputs — the Known / Inferred / Unknown discipline.
- Every scenario carries its own invalidation, not a single global one.
- Entry zones are conditional on stated inputs; nothing rests on an invented price.
- The decision is handed back to the human. The skill informs; it does not advise.
